================================================================================
8. HYPER TRADE SCORE
================================================================================

The ranking overlay is hyper_trade_score.

The score is 0-10 and is designed to rank explosive short-term repricing
potential, not company quality.

Formula:

    0.20 * premarket_repricing_energy
  + 0.18 * catalyst_violence
  + 0.17 * opening_drive_potential
  + 0.15 * attention_velocity
  + 0.10 * earlyness_score
  + 0.08 * float_squeeze_factor
  + 0.07 * level_clarity
  + 0.05 * source_quality
  - 0.10 * exhaustion_penalty
  - 0.08 * dilution_or_offering_penalty
  - 0.06 * fake_hype_penalty
  - 0.05 * mega_cap_boring_penalty

Component meanings:

premarket_repricing_energy:
    gap size, premarket dollar volume, spread quality, execution readiness

catalyst_violence:
    how capable the catalyst is of forcing immediate repricing

opening_drive_potential:
    asymmetry plus attention plus market overlay strength

attention_velocity:
    social/X/Reddit/StockTwits-style acceleration

earlyness_score:
    fresh story, fresh move, not already fully crowded/exhausted

float_squeeze_factor:
    low-float/squeeze/short-interest language plus attention

level_clarity:
    clear prior close/premarket price/spread/volume reference

source_quality:
    official/structured/credible source support

exhaustion_penalty:
    crowded/parabolic/late move, especially big gap with weak stage score

dilution_or_offering_penalty:
    offering, ATM, S-1/S-3, registered direct, reverse split, dilution

fake_hype_penalty:
    social-only hype without official/structured support

mega_cap_boring_penalty:
    AAPL/AMZN/AMD/GOOG/GOOGL/META/MSFT/NFLX/NVDA/PLTR/QQQ/SMCI/SPY/TSLA
    are penalized unless catalyst and attention are exceptional.
