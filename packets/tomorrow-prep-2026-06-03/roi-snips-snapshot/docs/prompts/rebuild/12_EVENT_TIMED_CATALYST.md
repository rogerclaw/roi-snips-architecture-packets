# 12 Event-Timed Catalyst

Track later-day and next-day catalysts through official company pages, SEC, news, X/Grok or X search, StockTwits, and price/volume reaction.

Classify outcomes as `BULLISH_CONFIRMED`, `NEUTRAL`, `DISAPPOINTING`, `NO_UPDATE`, or `RUMOR_ONLY`. Actions are shadow decisions until mode and guards explicitly permit otherwise.
