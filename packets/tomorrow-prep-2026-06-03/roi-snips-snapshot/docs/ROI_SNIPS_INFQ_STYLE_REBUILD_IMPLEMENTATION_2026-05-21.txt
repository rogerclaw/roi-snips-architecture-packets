## New opening-bell predicate list

`OPENING_BURST_HYPER_LONG` evaluates:
- `hyper_trade_score_ok`
- `opening_drive_score_ok`
- `volume_burst_ok`

- `ORB_OR_VWAP_FALLBACK`

## How BUY_NOW proposals are generated

1. Opening stream supervisor receives Alpaca quote/trade events.
2. `OpeningTapeTracker` updates raw tape and computes 5s/10s/15s/30s/60s features.
3. `evaluate_opening_burst_signal()` returns `BUY_NOW` only when hard blockers are absent and all opening-burst predicates pass.
4. Supervisor builds a deterministic proposal with `OPENING_BURST_HYPER_LONG`, aggressive limit price, entry cap, stop/thesis break, targets, notional, and `opening_exit_manager_armed=true`.
5. Order router still owns broker submission and checks guards, broker/account/data, duplicate/open-position state, risk validation, preview, and live/paper env arming.

## Fast cancel behavior

`should_fast_cancel()` cancels if:
- tape state is `HALT_OR_NO_QUOTE`, `SPREAD_EXPLODED`, `STALE_DATA`, `DRIVE_FAILED`, or `GAP_AND_CRAP`
- bid collapse flag fires
- price moves above entry cap before fill
- order remains unfilled while tape weakens
- replace limit is exceeded

`opening_position_action()` exits after fill on thesis break, bid collapse, stale/halt data, drive failure, gap-and-crap, time-stop with no continuation, or tightens/takes profit at target.

## How mega-cap fallback is demoted

`rank_clusters_for_research()` applies `mega_cap_fallback_audit` to common fallback names. Non-exceptional mega-caps receive:
- lower `research_priority_score`
- lower `hyper_trade_score`
- note `megacap_fallback_audit=demoted_nonexceptional`

A mega-cap passes only if catalyst strength, hyper score, attention, and exceptional lane tags are all present. Generic AMD-style filing noise is demoted and regression-tested against INFQ.
