CURRENT ROI SNIPS RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE AND HOW A STOCK IS FOUND
Date: 2026-05-20

Purpose
Roi Snips is built to find one high-conviction, long-only U.S. stock or ETF idea for a same-day to 1-3 day speculative trade. It is not supposed to produce a generic conservative watchlist. The target style is a volatile, catalyst-driven setup with asymmetric upside, strong evidence, live tape confirmation, and clear invalidation.

Hard constraints
- U.S. stocks and ETFs only.
- Long only.
- No options.
- No shorting.
- No margin behavior.
- One open position max.
- Intraday execution posture.
- Valid entry window: 09:30 ET to 11:00 ET.
- Opening-drive entries are only allowed from 09:30:00 to 09:34:59 ET.
- ORB_BREAK and VWAP_RECLAIM are allowed after 09:35 ET if the setup confirms.
- Force flat by 15:45 ET.
- LLM research can recommend and explain, but deterministic guards decide whether live execution is allowed.

Current scheduled research flow
The workflow is configured around repeated premarket passes:
- 04:05-04:25 ET: official sweep.
- 06:05-06:20 ET: filing refresh.
- 07:05-08:20 ET: tradeability gate.
- 08:20-09:00 ET: shortlist verification.
- 09:05-09:20 ET: final judge.
- 09:20-09:29 ET: bell-readiness check.
- 09:29 ET: freeze normal research changes except material breaking news.

Primary source lanes
The automated research pipeline uses scouts under `roi-snips/src/research/scouts/`:
- SEC / EDGAR filings via `sec_scout.py`.
- Company IR and press releases via `ir_scout.py`.
- Structured news via `newswire_scout.py` and configured Benzinga/Alpaca news adapters.
- FDA / biotech catalysts via `fda_scout.py`.
- Government / contract / regulatory catalysts via `government_scout.py`.
- Exchange / unusual listing or market structure signals via `exchange_scout.py`.
- Social and retail discussion signals via `social_scout.py`.
- Obscure or secondary confirmation via `obscure_scout.py`.

Market data and tradeability lanes
Roi Snips overlays market data before a candidate can become actionable:
- last or premarket price
- gap percentage
- premarket volume and dollar volume
- average 20-day dollar volume
- estimated spread
- execution readiness score
- execution blockers and warnings

The configured execution thresholds include:
- minimum execution price around $3 in workflow config, $2 in risk config
- minimum average dollar volume of $10,000,000
- maximum spread of 0.75% for normal execution
- tighter opening-drive spread limit of 35 bps
- minimum first-minute opening-drive volume of 150,000 shares
- minimum first-minute opening-drive dollar volume of $750,000
- maximum opening-drive chase of 1.0%
- stale quote limit of 2 seconds

How the automated pipeline finds candidates
1. Collect raw discovery events.
   The system gathers fresh filings, company announcements, news, FDA/government items, market-moving themes, retail/social acceleration, and obscure catalyst hints.

2. Derive a candidate universe.
   It turns raw events into a limited ticker universe, currently capped around 40 discovery candidates unless manually overridden.

3. Collect evidence for the candidate universe.
   Once tickers are discovered, the system does a second evidence pass across SEC, IR, news, FDA, exchange, government, social, and obscure lanes.

4. Cluster evidence by ticker and story.
   Related events are grouped into candidate clusters so a stock with a stacked catalyst gets treated as one thesis instead of scattered snippets.

5. Score research priority.
   The ranking model weighs:
   - catalyst strength
   - freshness
   - attention acceleration
   - asymmetry
   - official and structured confirmation
   - evidence diversity
   - source quality
   - obscurity / lesser-known-name bonus
   - crowding penalty
   - social-only penalty
   - mega-cap penalty unless the catalyst is genuinely exceptional

6. Apply execution gates.
   A candidate can rank well as research but still be blocked for execution if it lacks market overlay data, has broken spread/liquidity, lacks official or structured confirmation, has stale data, is too extended, or fails readiness thresholds.

7. Build the daily best-pick packet.
   If governed deep research/deep-mini succeeds, its output becomes the structured best-pick packet. If it fails or is unavailable, the system falls back to internal ranking and marks the packet as an internal fallback.

8. Generate reports.
   Outputs are written under:
   - `roi-snips/reports/morning/json/`
   - `roi-snips/reports/morning/md/`
   - per-run artifacts under `roi-snips/runs/`

How the LLM layer is supposed to be used
The LLM is not supposed to be the whole stock picker. It is a synthesis and judgment layer over evidence.

Preferred LLM use:
- Broad discovery: full deep research when several serious candidates exist.
- Shortlist synthesis: bounded deep-mini or equivalent once candidates are narrowed.
- Final memo: decisive single-name recommendation with entry framework, targets, thesis break, and sell triggers.
- Critique: explain why the chosen name beats other visible gappers.

The current prompt asks for:
- one single best stock, not a generic watchlist
- 2-4 ranked backups when useful
- catalyst and why it can move now
- why the market may not have fully priced it
- official evidence and sentiment trend
- buy range or entry framework
- same-day target
- 1-3 day target
- downside / thesis-break level
- monitoring timeframe
- sell triggers and danger signals

Manual browser-research path I use in chat
When doing this manually in chat, I use a similar architecture but with browser tools:
1. Search for current high-volume / high-catalyst candidates from news, filings, earnings, FDA, M&A, contracts, and market movers.
2. Cross-check official sources first: company release, SEC filing, FDA page, government contract notice, earnings deck, or exchange notice.
3. Check structured market/news sources for whether the catalyst is actually being picked up.
4. Check retail/social signals only as an amplifier: Reddit, StockTwits, X discussion velocity, forums, and trading blogs.
5. Reject or downgrade names that are rumor-only, stale, already exhausted, too illiquid, too wide-spread, or likely dilution traps.
6. Compare finalists on catalyst strength, freshness, asymmetry, tape confirmation, crowding, and invalidation clarity.
7. Produce one best pick with a concrete entry framework and exit rules.

Where Grok would fit
Grok is most interesting as a scout, not as the final authority.

Good use for Grok:
- X-search and real-time retail sentiment discovery.
- Fast detection of ticker chatter acceleration.
- Finding posts, threads, or narratives that normal web/news search misses.
- Acting as one more independent candidate-discovery lane.

Bad use for Grok:
- Letting X hype become proof by itself.
- Allowing Grok to choose a stock without official-source verification.
- Allowing Grok to override deterministic liquidity, spread, risk, or stale-data gates.
- Treating Grok as a reliable execution signal.

If added, Grok should be wired as:
1. X/social scout proposes candidates and sentiment summaries.
2. Roi Snips requires official or structured confirmation before promotion.
3. Existing ranking weighs Grok/X as social attention, not primary evidence.
4. Final LLM synthesis can cite Grok-derived sentiment only with source context.
5. Execution gates remain unchanged.

What "good" looks like for Charles's target style
A good pick should usually have:
- fresh hard catalyst
- official or structured confirmation
- believable reason the market has not fully priced it
- high relative volume or premarket participation
- small/mid-cap or lesser-known asymmetry when possible
- social/retail attention accelerating, but not as the only proof
- tight enough spread to trade
- clear level where the thesis is broken
- clear profit-taking and danger signals

What should be avoided
- boring mega-cap default picks unless the catalyst is exceptional
- blue-chip quality bias masquerading as short-term opportunity
- pure meme/social-only moves with no hard evidence
- already-exhausted gap-and-fade traps
- names with broken liquidity or untradeable spreads
- dilution/offering traps
- stale catalysts already digested by the market

Bottom line
The current architecture is catalyst-first discovery, evidence clustering, research ranking, market overlay, execution gating, and LLM synthesis. The best-stock decision should come from a combination of official catalyst evidence, attention acceleration, tradeable market structure, and clear risk thresholds. Grok could improve the social/X discovery lane, but it should not replace official verification or deterministic execution guards.
