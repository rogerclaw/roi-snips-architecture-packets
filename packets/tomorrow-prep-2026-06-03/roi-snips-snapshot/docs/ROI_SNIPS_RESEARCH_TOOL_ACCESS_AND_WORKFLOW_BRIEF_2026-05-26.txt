5. Research workflow I will run for stock selection
---------------------------------------------------
This is the intended operational sequence for a serious Roi Snips morning research pass.

Step 1: Preflight and state check
- Read Roi Snips active ledger.
- Check whether the day is market-open, weekend, holiday, or practice mode.
- Check runtime/config status without exposing secrets.
- Confirm whether the task is research-only, brokerless validation, paper, or live-armed.
- Fail closed if authorization/state is ambiguous.

Step 2: Broad catalyst discovery
- Search for same-day and recent catalysts across official and structured sources.
- Focus on catalysts that can move a stock over hours to 1-3 trading days.
- Prioritize:
  - FDA/medical approvals or trial updates
  - regulatory wins
  - government/contract awards
  - M&A, strategic reviews, takeover speculation with evidence
  - partnerships/licensing
  - product launches
  - tech breakthroughs
  - earnings surprises
  - guidance revisions
  - unusual volume/RVOL
  - short-squeeze/float dynamics
  - sector momentum
  - policy-theme runners

Step 3: Source validation
- Map each candidate to source lanes:
  - official/primary
  - structured financial/news
  - social/retail overlay
- Preserve validation status instead of flattening all evidence into one score.
- Penalize unverified hype, stale catalysts, fake/rehashed news, weak liquidity, and names that are already fully crowded.

Step 4: Candidate normalization
- Normalize ticker, company, catalyst type, timestamp/freshness, source URLs, evidence quality, price/liquidity notes, and social overlay.
- De-duplicate repeated references to the same catalyst.
- Identify whether the setup is the actual research leader or just a tradable fallback.

Step 5: Scoring
- Apply INFQ-style hard-catalyst scoring.
- Apply hyper-trade score.
- Apply liquidity and marketability checks.
- Apply speculative risk penalties.
- Apply anti-chase and lifecycle state:
  - premarket building
  - opening drive
  - already extended
  - second-leg watch
  - no-trade extended
- Score same-style backups separately from boring mega-cap defaults.

Step 6: Research leader vs executable primary
- The best research candidate can remain research_leader even if it is not immediately buyable.
- executable_primary should be promoted only when execution-readiness passes.
- A candidate can be "best story, not buyable here" if it is already too extended.
- This distinction is central to avoiding the old failure mode where a routine default name displaced the actual asymmetric catalyst.

Step 7: Optional governed deep-research escalation
- Use only after there is a bounded shortlist.
- Build a structured shortlist brief with the candidate tickers, catalysts, evidence, unresolved questions, decision criteria, and requested output.
- Run the governed deep-research runner.
- Let the architect choose no_escalation, deep_mini, or deep.
- Include response/request IDs if escalated and completed.
- Treat deep output as another evidence layer, not as permission to trade.

Step 8: Final ranking and packet generation
- Produce one decisive best pick or explicitly say no high-quality long.
- Include ranked backups, not a generic watchlist.
- Required final packet fields:
  - chosen ticker and company
  - exact catalyst
  - why it can move sharply
  - why the market may not have fully priced it
  - evidence reviewed
  - official-vs-structured-vs-social evidence split
  - sentiment/discussion trend
  - catalyst classification
  - buy zone or no-chase range
  - same-day upside target
  - 1-3 day upside target
  - thesis-break/downside level
  - monitoring window
  - profit-taking triggers
  - danger signals
  - tradeability/liquidity notes
  - opening-drive vs post-09:35 framework
  - ORB/VWAP observation levels when data permits
  - no-trade alternatives and why they are rejected
  - execution-risk notes for basic brokerage tools
  - deep research route/result IDs when used

Step 9: Opening-market observation
- For research-only or no-order validation, observe and report only.
- For any future armed mode, deterministic gates still control execution.
- Opening frameworks include:
  - opening drive
  - opening burst
  - ORB break
  - VWAP reclaim
  - premarket-high reclaim
  - second-leg continuation
- The system must keep order/risk gates separate from research enthusiasm.

Step 10: Artifact capture
- Save reports under Roi Snips report directories.
- Keep logs for research architecture and deep-research decisions.
- Preserve final summary metrics for validation runs.
- If a run is brokerless/no-order, report that explicitly.
