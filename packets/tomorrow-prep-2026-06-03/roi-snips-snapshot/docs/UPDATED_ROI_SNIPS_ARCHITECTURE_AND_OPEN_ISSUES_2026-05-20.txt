UPDATED ROI SNIPS ARCHITECTURE AND OPEN ISSUES
Generated: 2026-05-20

Purpose
=======

This is the current practical architecture for Roi Snips after the Alpaca,
OpenAI/deep-research, Grok/X, and Reddit setup work.

The goal is still one decisive short-term long-only U.S. stock/ETF pick for a
same-day to 1-3 day speculative trade. It should not behave like a generic
watchlist generator. It should behave like a fast catalyst hunter with hard
execution gates.

The system should combine:

1. deterministic source scouts
2. evidence ledger and source scoring
3. market/tape/liquidity overlay
4. social attention overlay
5. governed online AI research, comparable in shape to a ChatGPT Pro search
6. execution/no-trade gates that outrank all LLM enthusiasm
7. a final packet Charles can actually act on


Current High-Level Architecture
===============================

One-line version:

    broad catalyst discovery
    -> evidence ledger
    -> social/attention overlay
    -> market/tape overlay
    -> reject bad setups
    -> rank finalists
    -> governed online AI research on the shortlist
    -> one final best-pick/no-trade packet
    -> live execution only if all deterministic gates pass and live entries are armed


Research Flow
=============

1. Discovery scouts build the universe
--------------------------------------

Current usable or partly usable lanes:

- Alpaca SIP quote/trade/bar market data
- Alpaca account/order/position checks
- Alpaca News
- Benzinga newswire
- SEC EDGAR
- Company IR / press-release discovery
- FDA / biotech keyword scout
- Government / contract keyword scout
- Exchange / mover scout
- Reddit public JSON fallback
- optional X API adapter when X bearer token is present
- Grok-backed X/web search through OpenClaw
- Governed OpenAI deep-research runner

Discovery should include obvious premarket movers, fresh hard catalysts,
official filings, obscure under-covered catalysts, social attention spikes,
and candidates that look volatile enough to matter for a small speculative
same-day / 1-3 day trade.

2. Evidence ledger normalizes every candidate
---------------------------------------------

For every ticker, the system should track:

- ticker and company mapping
- catalyst claim
- official source URLs
- structured/news source URLs
- social/X/Reddit citations
- freshness
- source quality
- official confirmation count
- structured confirmation count
- social confirmation count
- story stage: early, developing, late, crowded, exhausted
- attention acceleration
- crowding / pump risk
- hidden-edge or why-not-fully-priced rationale
- unresolved questions
- elimination flags

Social can discover candidates. Social cannot prove candidates.

3. Ranking scores the research opportunity
------------------------------------------

The ranking model should prefer:

- fresh hard catalysts
- official or structured confirmation
- strong asymmetry
- early story stage
- unusual but still tradeable attention/volume
- smaller or lesser-known names when the catalyst quality is comparable

The ranking model should penalize:

- mega-cap defaults unless the catalyst/tape is exceptional
- stale catalysts
- hype-only setups
- exhausted/parabolic moves
- missing official or structured evidence
- poor liquidity
- wide spreads
- unclear ticker mapping

4. Market overlay and execution gates decide whether a research idea is tradable
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Roi Snips must separate "interesting idea" from "executable trade."

Market overlay checks:

- live quote
- last price
- bid/ask
- spread
- prior close
- premarket bars
- volume / dollar volume
- average liquidity
- halt/abnormal state
- price band
- configured data feed

Execution gates block candidates when:

- no official or structured confirmation exists
- quote/last/bid/ask/spread data is missing
- spread is too wide
- liquidity is too thin
- price band fails
- session phase is wrong
- one-position rule fails
- broker/account state is unhealthy
- kill switch is active
- disable-new-entries is active

If nothing passes execution gates, the correct output is no-trade or
research-leader-only. It must not invent an executable best pick.


ChatGPT Pro-Style Online Research Route
=======================================

Charles's concern is correct: this system needs a route that behaves like a
real online ChatGPT Pro search/deep-research pass, not just a local scoring
script or a normal LLM chat reply.

The intended route now is:

1. Deterministic scouts build a broad universe from market, news, filings,
   official sources, social, and mover feeds.

2. The pipeline ranks and filters candidates before the LLM sees them. This
   prevents the AI from wasting time on junk and prevents social hype from
   bypassing gates.

3. The top execution-eligible or near-eligible names are written into a
   generated shortlist brief.

4. `tools/deep-research-runner` sends that brief through the governed OpenAI
   research architect path.

5. The architect routes the request:

   - `no_escalation` for simple cases or smoke checks
   - `deep_mini` / `o4-mini-deep-research` for normal morning shortlist synthesis
   - `deep` / `o3-deep-research` for unusually broad, ambiguous, high-stakes,
     or source-conflicted cases

6. When escalated, the OpenAI Responses API can attach web-search tooling and
   produce an online research memo with citations.

7. The deep-research output must produce one best pick plus only enough backups
   to explain why the winner is better.

8. The final packet must include:

   - best ticker/company or explicit no-trade
   - catalyst
   - why the move may not be fully priced
   - evidence and sentiment/attention trend
   - buy zone / entry framework
   - same-day target
   - 1-3 day target
   - downside / thesis-break level
   - monitoring timeframe
   - profit-taking triggers
   - danger signals
   - why it beats backups
   - execution gate status
   - live-trading guard status

Important constraint: deep research can help choose and explain the idea. It
cannot override official-source requirements, spread/liquidity gates, stale
data checks, one-position rule, kill switch, disable-entry state, or the
long-only/no-options/no-shorts/no-margin constraints.


Grok / X Search Role
====================

Grok is now usable as a first-pass X/social discovery lane.

Current working shape:

- OpenClaw has Grok web search available through provider `grok`.
- `src/adapters/grok_search.py` calls OpenClaw web search instead of storing
  xAI credentials in Roi Snips.
- `src/research/scouts/social_scout.py` emits `grok_x_search` raw events.
- Those events are marked social-only:
  - `social_flag=true`
  - `official_flag=false`
  - `structured_flag=false`
- Existing execution gates therefore still block Grok-only candidates from
  executable status until another source lane confirms them.

What Grok is good for:

- finding X/cashtag chatter
- finding retail momentum and attention acceleration
- surfacing obscure tickers getting sudden discussion
- adding citations/URLs to the social overlay

What Grok is not allowed to do:

- choose the final trade by itself
- populate an order plan directly
- override official/structured source requirements
- override market data, spread, liquidity, or risk gates

Remaining Grok work:

- tune query templates for premarket small-cap catalyst discovery
- persist full response JSON, query text, and citations more richly in run
  artifacts
- optionally move from Grok web-search wrapping to direct xAI `x_search`
  tooling if that gives better source control

How This All Works Together
===========================

The architecture should work as a layered funnel:

1. Broad scouts find candidates
   - official/company/SEC/news/FDA/government/exchange/Alpaca/Benzinga
   - social lanes: Reddit fallback, X optional, Grok/X

2. Evidence ledger dedupes and scores candidates
   - official and structured sources carry proof weight
   - social sources carry attention weight only

3. Market overlay tests whether the idea is actually tradable
   - spread, liquidity, price, bars, prior close, halt/session state

4. Deterministic gates split candidates into:
   - executable candidates
   - research leaders that are not executable
   - rejected/no-trade names

5. Governed OpenAI deep research analyzes the shortlist
   - this is the ChatGPT Pro-style online research layer
   - it should compare candidates, verify sources, and choose one best idea

6. Final packet is produced
   - one pick or explicit no-trade
   - full catalyst/evidence/entry/target/risk packet
   - execution gate and live guard state included

7. Live monitor can only act if explicitly armed
   - disable-new-entries must be cleared intentionally
   - no social-only or blocked candidate can become an order
